## 3D icon review (2026-11-14)

All 13 icons were rendered from the CI-generated GLBs and reviewed. Four were wrong and have been fixed in `create_3d_icon_pack.py`:

- Play: the triangle was buried inside the disc.
- Medal: the star was buried inside the disc.
- Book: did not read as a book; now a closed storybook.
- Puzzle piece: was a box with two spheres; now an extruded jigsaw piece.

Only Baby Lion has animation clips (`Idle`, `Celebrate`); the other models are static.

## Risks

- **Emoji rendering.** Content is limited to Emoji 11 (Android 9+). On Android 7–8 (minSdk 24) some pictures may not render.
- **Release signing.** Release builds still sign with the debug key until the owner supplies a keystore.
- **Device testing.** No physical-device testing yet; Flutter Scene GPU rendering is unverified on hardware.
- **Multiple children.** All progress is shared by one profile.
